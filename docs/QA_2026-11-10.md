## Defects

1. **Header always shows "Not signed in"; no identity, groups or Sign out button (blocks sign-out). Severity high.**
   - Where: `apps/web/src/components/Nav.tsx` and `apps/web/src/lib/schemas.ts`.
   - Expected: the header shows alice @acme, the analyst role, the finance and analysts groups, and a Sign out button, and the Chat and Documents links appear.
   - Actual: every page shows "Not signed in" even though the token is stored and the pages work. There is no Sign out button, and no Chat or Documents nav links. Step 1's expectation (header shows alice with finance, analysts) fails.
   - Cause (verified): `Principal.org_id` uses `z.uuid()`. With zod 4.6.7, `z.uuid()` rejects `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001` (the seeded org id) because it requires a valid version and variant nibble. I confirmed this with `safeParse`, which returns false. `GET /me` returns 200 with that id, so `getMe` fails validation and `Nav` falls back to `setMe(null)`.
   - Suggested fix: use `z.guid()` or `z.string()`.
   - My script worked around the missing Sign out button by clearing localStorage and reloading `/`.
2. **Out-of-scope or irrelevant questions get a confident 90% "grounded" answer. Severity medium.**
   - Where: bob's step 5.
   - Expected: abstain, or a low-confidence answer, when the retrieved chunks do not address the question. bob's 8-Ks do not mention supply-chain risk, and the reranker scores are all negative.
   - Actual: the answer is boilerplate stitched from 8-K headers ("UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION Washington, D.C. [1]...") at 90% confidence and "grounded".
   - Likely cause: the verifier is the "fake" model (shown on the card), so grounding checks are not meaningful in this environment.
3. **The UI never shows the `abstained` flag. Severity low.**
   - Where: `AnswerCard`. The flag is visible only in the replay checkpoints.
4. **The Replay page's first two checkpoints are duplicates. Severity low.**
   - Where: the Replay page, steps -1 and 0, both named `__start__`.
   - Expected: one start checkpoint per turn. Actual: two, differing only in `next`. This is LangGraph's normal checkpoint output, so it may be acceptable.
5. **The Documents table runs the form and metadata together. Severity low.**
   - Where: /documents. "10-K" is concatenated with "cik=..." and no separator appears in the text content, for example "10-Kcik=789019".
   - The visual layout may be fine (see `03-alice-documents.png`).
6. **The HITL tool title is derived clumsily from the request. Severity low.**
   - Where: step 7. Request: "Please create a ticket to review Apple's 10-K risk section". Ticket title: "to review Apple's 10-K risk section". The leading "to" is a parsing artefact.
